About Tafo Golf Centre

Welcome to Tafo Golf Centre, a place for golf enthusiasts, beginners and visitors who want to enjoy the game of golf.


Our goal is to promote golf, encourage sporting excellence and provide an enjoyable environment for golfers and the community.
